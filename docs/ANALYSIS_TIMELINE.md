1. Freeze rebuilt seed-23 inputs, checkpoints, embedding policy, probes, and comparison arms.
2. Evaluate the four reciprocal functional cells at seed 23.
3. Apply the continuation rule defined by the 1 August amendment.
4. Do not authorize additional confirmatory seeds when the gate is not met.
5. Treat later species-disjoint and comparative analyses as post-hoc sensitivity or interpretation layers.

## Required reporting distinctions

- `Tree − Base` asks whether the Tree-adapted checkpoint differs from the unadapted Base checkpoint.
- `Tree − strongest equal-budget plant control` asks whether the Tree corpus has a unique advantage over another continuation corpus with comparable budget.
- A positive `Tree − Base` point estimate can coexist with failure of the equal-budget superiority gate.
- A value below `+0.02` is not automatically within the ROPE. ROPE membership must be checked against both boundaries.
- A bootstrap replicate proportion above a threshold is not a posterior probability unless a probability model explicitly supports that interpretation.
